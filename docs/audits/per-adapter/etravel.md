# Audit: etravel (high)

## Shrnutí

src/sources/etravel.ts does one categories-discovery call to https://www.etravel.cz/api/searchapi/getsearchresult?tt=1, resolves 12 hardcoded country names to pipe-joined region ids, then issues one getsearchresult per country (ds=0&tt=1&d=<ids>&dd=today&rd=today+60d&nn=7|8|...|14&ac1=2&kc1=0&ic1=0) and maps tours[] via the shared mapDerTours() in src/sources/der.ts. Live run (2026-07-29, residential CZ IP, Chrome UA): 226 offers in 93 s, price/person min 6 902 / median 25 800 / max 76 400 CZK. The mapping itself is genuinely good — I verified three offers against their own pages and the price/date/nights/board/country/stars are exact, and the computed claimedDiscountPct matches eTravel's own on-site badge to the percent. The problem is volume and one dropped field. Every country returns exactly 20 rows because the API's default page size is 20 and the adapter never paginates, even though the same endpoint paginates trivially via pitg/pstg (verified: pitg=100&pstg=0 returns 100). So it ingests 226 of the 5 593 tours available inside its own filter (4.0 %), always the 20 cheapest per country. On top of that, 66 of the site's 78 country categories are never queried at all — including Španělsko, which with 2 313 tours is a bigger destination on eTravel than Řecko, plus Bulharsko/Itálie/Kypr/Chorvatsko (all four named in the user's own leto-more profile) and Seychely/Tanzánie/Indonésie/Kapverdy (named in the exotika profile). Finally, departureAirport is hardcoded null while the API carries it, and because the result is price-sorted the cheapest-20 slice is dominated by non-Czech departures: all 20 Kuba offers leave from Vienna, 19/20 Maledivy from Munich/Frankfurt, 16/20 Dominikánská republika from Frankfurt/Munich — shown on the board with no airport signal at all.

## Nálezy k opravě

### 1.

TRUNCATION — hard 20-per-country cap, no pagination. Every one of the 12 country queries returns exactly 20 tours (Vietnam 6 = its whole inventory) regardless of toursCount. Live toursCount inside the adapter's own filter: Řecko 2124, Turecko 1031, SAE 554, Egypt 455, Kuba 251, Mexiko 232, Maledivy 220, Dominikánská r. 203, Thajsko 195, Mauricius 166, Srí Lanka 156, Vietnam 6 = 5 593 tours available, 226 ingested (4.0 %). The API is price-ascending by default, so the slice is always the 20 cheapest: Řecko yields 6 902–12 658 CZK out of 2124 tours. Pagination exists and is trivial — the site's own "Dalších 20" button fires the same endpoint with pitg/pstg, and I confirmed https://www.etravel.cz/api/searchapi/getsearchresult?ds=0&tt=1&d=64419|...|64426&dd=2026-07-29&rd=2026-09-27&nn=7|8|9|10|11|12|13|14&ac1=2&pitg=100&pstg=0 returns 100 tours (11 322–17 390 CZK), and pstg=100 returns the next slice (17 490–17 990 CZK). Consequence for the board: discounted mid/upper-band offers are structurally invisible — in Egypt 12 of the cheapest 20 carried a discount, but there are 455 tours and the adapter never sees 435 of them.

### 2.

COVERAGE — 66 of eTravel's 78 country categories are never queried. TARGET_COUNTRIES in src/sources/etravel.ts lists 12 names; the categories response (getsearchresult?tt=1) returns 78 countries with live inventory. Untargeted countries with measured toursCount in the adapter's own 60-day/7-14-night/2-adult filter: Španělsko 2313 (cheapest 10 384 CZK — this is a LARGER destination on eTravel than Řecko's 2124 and the single biggest on the site), Bulharsko 517 (from 6 197 CZK — the cheapest inventory anywhere on the site, below the adapter's global minimum of 6 902), Itálie 428, Portugalsko 318, Kypr 314, Tunisko 206, Chorvatsko 196, Malta 81, Tanzánie 80 (this is where eTravel's Zanzibar sits), Indonésie 72, Albánie 43, Kapverdské ostrovy 17 — 4 585 tours across just these 12, zero ingested. This collides directly with config/watch.yaml: the leto-more profile asks for [Řecko, Turecko, Egypt, Španělsko, Kypr, Bulharsko, Chorvatsko, Itálie] and the adapter serves only 3 of those 8; the exotika profile asks for Seychely (50 tours live), Zanzibar/Tanzanie (80), Indonésie (72), Kapverdy (17) and Keňa — all present on eTravel, none queried. The etravel.ts comment justifying the omission of Zanzibar is correct that it is not a country-level category, but Tanzánie IS one and would cover it.

### 3.

FIELD — departureAirport is always null although the API carries it, and 86 of ~120 exotic offers actually depart from Vienna/Munich/Frankfurt. der.ts line 149 hardcodes `departureAirport: null`, but each tour has tour.flight.departure.segments[0].airport.{from,fromIATA,fromId} — e.g. {"from":"Praha","fromIATA":"PRG","fromId":4312} for Egypt, {"from":"Mnichov","fromIATA":"MUC","fromId":2397} for Maledivy. Because the adapter omits the `to=` (departure-airport) filter the frontend sends (to=4312|4305|2682|4308|4309|4392) and the result is price-sorted, cheaper foreign departures crowd out Czech ones. Measured airport composition of the exact 20 rows the adapter ingests per country: Kuba MUC/VIE {VIE:20} — every single Kuba offer on the board is a Vienna departure; Maledivy {MUC:16, FRA:3, PRG:1}; Mauricius {VIE:13, MUC:6, PRG:1}; Dominikánská r. {FRA:14, MUC:2, PRG:4}; Srí Lanka {MUC:9, PRG:11}; SAE {PRG:17, VIE:3}. Adding to=4312|4305|2682|4308|4309|4392 to the Maledivy query returns {PRG:20} with a cheapest price of 37 860 CZK vs the 35 549 CZK the adapter currently reports — i.e. the headline Maldives price on the board is ~2 300 CZK/person cheaper than anything actually bookable from Czechia, with nothing on the card saying so. Verified against the rendered page: https://www.etravel.cz/hotely/maledivy/kaafu-atol/kaafu/centara-ras-fushi-resort-a-spa?...&DD=2026-09-20 shows "Jak pojedete? Letecky - Mnichov" and a MUC→MLE flight. Czech departures also vary within Řecko (KOS BAY = Brno, ELEFTHERIA = Ostrava), so the field is meaningful even domestically.

### 4.

FIELD — tourOperator is always null although the API supplies it. der.ts line 157 hardcodes `tourOperator: null`, but every tour carries tour.tourOperator with real values: "TUI-CZ" (ALEXANDER THE GREAT RESORT, FLAMENCO BEACH), "EXIM Tours" (MAGIC BEACH BY AMARINA, Blue Lake Resort), "FISCHER" (all four sampled Maledivy resorts). eTravel is a reseller carrying several operators, so this is the field that would let the board tell an eTravel-listed Fischer package apart from the same package pulled by the fischer adapter — relevant for cross-source dedup/price comparison.

### 5.

DOCS — two stale claims in the source comments (no runtime impact). (a) src/sources/etravel.ts line 44 says fetchOffers does "one categories discovery request, then one getsearchresult request per target country (4 requests total)" — TARGET_COUNTRIES has had 12 entries since the exotika expansion, so it is 13 requests, and the live run took 93 s. (b) src/sources/der.ts lines 15-19 / etravel.ts lines 32-34 call eTravel "the only source in this project whose API surfaces an official Omnibus 30-day-minimum price (tour.price.lowestPrice)" and "the reference source for that field" — in the live run omnibusLowestPrice was null for 226/226 offers, and lowestPrice was null on every tour I inspected across Řecko, Egypt, Maledivy, Španělsko. The der.ts docstring already hedges this empirically; the etravel.ts header does not, and reads as if the field is populated.

### 6.

SCOPE (minor) — the fixed 60-day departure window plus nn=7..14 shapes what is visible. Winter exotics, which config/watch.yaml's exotika profile explicitly targets ("departure_months: [] — celoročně, hlavní sezóna je zima"), are never seen from a summer scan: Thajsko has 195 tours in the next 60 days but 324 for a 2026-12-01→2027-02-28 window. For Maledivy (220 vs 217) and Seychely (50 vs 52) the difference is negligible, so this is the smallest of the coverage issues — noted for completeness, not a defect on its own.

## Adversariální ověření

- **POTVRZENO** (high): CONFIRMED. Independently reproduced end to end.

CODE: src/sources/etravel.ts searchUrl() emits only ds/tt/d/dd/rd/er/isss/nn/ac1/kc1/ic1 — no paging param — and fetchOffers() does exactly one getsearchresult call per country, mapping res.tours once with no loop. grep for pitg/pstg across src/ returns zero hits.

LIVE (Chrome UA, ~4 requests total, 3 s gaps): ran the real adapter with a recording http stub that hit the network only for discovery + Egypt. It issued 13 URLs, none carrying a page param; Egypt returned tours.length=20 with toursCount=455, and the adapter emitted 20 offers (adultPrice 11321.6-13890).

PAGINATION PROVEN MYSELF: identical URL + pitg=100&pstg=0 returned 100 tours (11321.6-17390, toursCount still 455); pitg=20&pstg=100 returned the next 20 (17490-17990). Every slice is strictly price-ascending, so the default page genuinely is the 20 cheapest. The fix is a query param the site's own "Dalších 20" button uses.

CORROBORATION IN-REPO: the committed fixture tests/fixtures/etravel/getsearchresult.json is itself tours=20 / toursCount=2108 — the truncation was baked in at capture time. tests/fixtures/fischer/last-minute.html exposes the shared DER platform settings {"searchFromIndex":0,"toursCountToGet":20}, and the design spec's Exim row already documents "20/str., paging.RowsToSkip" — the platform's paging is known to this project, just never wired for etravel.

PRODUCTION EVIDENCE: logs/scan.log shows "etravel: fetched 226 offers" in all 107 recorded scans, invariant to the unit (11 countries x 20 + Vietnam 6). Every other source fluctuates with real inventory (zajezdy 291-312, fischer 12-50, firo 31-71). A pinned constant across 107 runs is a structural cap, not a market fact.

MATERIALITY: ranks 21-100 for Egypt alone hold 45 further discounted tours (claimed 35-56 %) across 79 hotels that never enter the corpus, so discounted mid-band offers are structurally invisible. Worse, src/core/discount.ts builds hotel/locality/market baselines from ingested offers (min 4/8/8 prices per bucket); feeding it only the cheapest ~4 % of each country biases those baselines downward and degrades real-discount classification, not merely coverage.

FALSE-POSITIVE CHECKS: (a) not a missing field; (b) not a teaser/term mismatch — same URL and params, only page size differs; (c) the module header documents the request-count design ("4 requests total", itself stale at 13) but nowhere states an intentional 20-item or cheapest-only cap; (d) dedupe by sourceOfferKey is within-run and cannot produce exactly 20 per country.

Severity high rather than critical: no crash, corruption, or security exposure, and other sources partially cover the same market — but this is silent, invariant loss of ~96 % of the inventory the adapter's own query matches, with a one-param fix.

DOWNSTREAM IMPACT (why high, not medium): departureAirport is a first-class product field, not decoration. web/src/lib/term.ts:91-95 renders the Odlet cell as "—" for every etravel row (transport is 'flight', so the 'vlastní'/'bus' fallbacks don't apply). web/src/lib/filters.ts:157-167 — selecting ANY airport chip drops all 226 etravel offers, and airportFacets() never lists them. src/core/format.ts:126 omits the ✈️ line from the live Telegram alert. Worst, src/core/normalize.ts:157-159 folds them into the `'*'` airport bucket of computeMatchKey, so a Vienna-departing etravel Kuba row can cross-source-merge with a Prague-departing row from another airport-less source — the spec (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-04-dovolenky-design.md §13) defines identity as "hotel × termín × strava × odlet" and normalizeAirport even maps Vídeň→VIE, i.e. the design anticipates foreign origins being distinguishable. Net effect: in a price-comparison product, the cheapest row per exotic country is systematically a MUC/VIE/FRA departure, shown as "—", and it feeds the notification path. The fix is reading one field that is already in every response.
