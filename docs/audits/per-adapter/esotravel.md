# Audit: esotravel (high)

## Shrnutí

`src/sources/esotravel.ts` does 11 plain GETs — 10 hardcoded country slugs `/dovolena/{thajsko,maledivy,mauritius,zanzibar,dominikanska-republika,sri-lanka,vietnam,kuba,mexiko,seychely}/zajezdy/zajezdy/` plus `/last-minute/` — and cheerio-parses `div.listview.primary` cards, taking price from `.visible .price strong`, dates from `.detail-date`, nights from `span.days`, stars from `h2 i.fa-star`, board from `.hidden .popis i.fa-utensils`, country from the hardcoded slug (or `.tour-type span` on last-minute). It ran green: 62 offers, 11/11 pages OK, prices 32 490 / 66 990 / 678 300 CZK (min/median/max), zero domestic junk, zero NULL departureDate/nights at the adapter boundary. The prices it does return are correct and genuinely per person — I verified three offers against their own pages, including the 678 300 CZK outlier, which is REAL, not a parse error or group price. The problem is volume and two field defects. The adapter never paginates: ESO's "Načíst další" is an AJAX GET of the same URL with `?strana=N` (see `/js/redesign/pageloader.js`), each feed panel is capped at 15 cards, and the module docstring asserts the exact opposite ("only reveals already-embedded cards (no extra request)"). Combined with the 10-slug list (the sitemap has 118 country slugs), the adapter sees 60 of ≥263 priced offers the site publishes — 23%. Separately, for season-priced stays it invents a departure date out of a price-validity window ("V období 01. 05. – 31. 10. 2026" → 2026-05-01, three months in the past) which is the entire Maldives block and exactly explains the DB's departureDate-NULL 24%.

## Nálezy k opravě

### 1.

COVERAGE, root cause 1 — NO PAGINATION; the module docstring is factually wrong. `/js/redesign/pageloader.js` implements `a.load-more` as `$.ajax({url: path + '?strana=' + page})`, slicing `<!-- startresults {feedId} --> … <!-- endresults -->` out of the response and stopping on `<!-- feed_end -->`. Each feed panel serves 15 cards per page. Proof: `https://www.esotravel.cz/dovolena/maledivy/zajezdy/zajezdy/?strana=1` returns HTTP 200 with 15 FURTHER cards, zero URL overlap with page 0, including three priced Maldives resorts the adapter never sees — Constance Moofushi 129 490 Kc (`/pobytove/maledivy/hotel/constance-moofushi-maledivy-jizni-ari-atol/?ha=1786&zj=470`), Dhigali 132 990 Kc, Lily Beach 179 990 Kc. `https://www.esotravel.cz/dovolena/thajsko/zajezdy/zajezdy/?strana=1` returns 15 further cards, ALL priced, and no `feed_end` marker (more pages still). The adapter's comment at src/sources/esotravel.ts:9-10 ("the 'Načíst další' button only reveals already-embedded cards (no extra request), so a single GET per listing sees everything") is the assumption that breaks everything else.

### 2.

COVERAGE, measured — adapter sees 23% of ESO's bookable inventory. Walking the site's own two global feeds (`/pobytove/zajezdy/?strana=0..10` → 158 priced cards, then priced cards stop; `/poznavaci/?strana=0..6` → 105 priced cards, then priced cards stop) yields 263 distinct priced offers. The adapter's 62 offers overlap 60 of them → 203 priced offers (77%) never reach the board. Missed offers by URL country slug: thajsko 114, usa 11, francouzska-polynesie 10, australie 7, antarktida 3, indie 3, jizni-afrika 3, cina 3, kanada 3, maledivy 3, japonsko 2, kena 2, nepal 2, namibie 2, egypt 2, ekvador 2, argentina 2, kolumbie 2, zimbabwe 2, plus singletons for jizni-pol, spicberky, gronsko, bhutan, chile, novy-zeland, brazilie, panama, zambie, uganda, peru, botswana, kostarika, jizni-korea, mosambik, tchaj-wan, malajsie, oman, kambodza, maroko, saudska-arabie. Thajsko alone loses 114 stay offers purely to the 15-card page cap (the adapter returns 21 Thai offers; the site has ~135 priced).

### 3.

COVERAGE, root cause 2 — the hardcoded LISTINGS array (src/sources/esotravel.ts:88-101) is 10 of the 118 country slugs in `https://www.esotravel.cz/sitemap.xml`, and it is skewed to beach destinations that are currently sold out. Live card counts on the adapter's own URLs: mauritius 17 cards / 1 priced (16 x `vyprodano`), zanzibar 2 cards / 0 priced, seychely 19 cards / 2 priced → the adapter yields 1 Mauricius, 0 Zanzibar, 0 Seychely offers. Meanwhile uncovered slugs are alive: `/dovolena/usa/zajezdy/` 15/15 priced, `/dovolena/karibik/zajezdy/` 30/30 priced, `/dovolena/jizni-afrika/zajezdy/` 8 cards / 4 priced. Also missing is the whole top price band: the site's 10 offers >=200 000 Kc are all invisible (highest `od 2 767 000 Kc`, `/poznavaci/jizni-pol/srdce-antarktidy-jizni-pol-v-luxusni-variante/?termin=2249728`; 429 000 Antarktida; 264 990 Australie; 202 990 Bora Bora). Site price distribution 20-50k:32, 50-100k:171, 100-200k:50, >=200k:10 vs adapter 20-50k:18, 50-100k:39, >=100k:5.

### 4.

FIELD, departureDate is fabricated for season-priced stays — this is the DB's "departureDate NULL 24%". On `https://www.esotravel.cz/dovolena/maledivy/zajezdy/zajezdy/` all 15 pobytove cards carry the identical `.detail-date` "01. 05. - 31. 10. 2026", and the offer's own page labels that row "V období 01. 05. - 31. 10. 2026 | 10 dní / 7 nocí | 121 990,-" (`https://www.esotravel.cz/pobytove/maledivy/hotel/constance-halaveli-maledivy-severni-ari-atol/?ha=1787&zj=470`, tab Termíny a ceny — four such season rows: 01.05.-31.10.2026 = 121 990, 01.11.-22.12.2026 = 182 490, 23.12.2026-10.01.2027 = 308 990, 11.01.-30.04.2027 = 184 990). It is a price-validity window, not a term. `parseFirstDate` takes the range start, so the adapter emits departureDate 2026-05-01 for all 15 Maldives offers — 2.9 months in the PAST on the 2026-07-29 scan date — which is 15/62 = 24% of output and is exactly what commit 4bcfbdf then nulls at ingest. Note this is product-specific, not universal: Thai pobytove cards on `/dovolena/thajsko/zajezdy/zajezdy/` do show a genuine term (30. 10. - 13. 11. 2026) and map correctly.

### 5.

FIELD, country NULL 18% is recoverable and the fallback also mis-assigns. All 11 NULL-country offers come from `/last-minute/`, where `.tour-type span` holds "USA"/"Čína" — absent from COUNTRY_BY_KEY in src/core/normalize.ts, so `isKnownCountry` rejects them and country stays null (e.g. `https://www.esotravel.cz/poznavaci/usa/velky-okruh-havajskymi-ostrovy/?termin=2262546`, `https://www.esotravel.cz/poznavaci/cina/velky-okruh-cinou/?termin=2249885`). Every one of them has the country in its own URL path (`/poznavaci/usa/`, `/poznavaci/cina/`). The inverse error also exists: cross-listed promo cards blindly inherit the listing slug — `https://www.esotravel.cz/africka-odysea/` is tagged country=Mauricius, locality=Afrika although its page describes Maroko → Namibie → Botswana → Zimbabwe → Seychely, and `https://www.esotravel.cz/poznavaci/kambodza/tajuplny-angkor-wat-s-odpocinkem-na-plazich-v-thajsku/?termin=2263804` is tagged Thajsko. 13 of 62 offers disagree with their own URL slug.

### 6.

FIELD, board unknown 76% — confirmed real, and recoverable one level down. `.popis i.fa-utensils` exists only on the Maldives-shaped feed (15/15 cards there → BB 2, FB 7, HB 2, AI 3, none 1 = exactly the 15 non-unknown boards in the run). `/dovolena/thajsko/zajezdy/zajezdy/` contains ZERO `fa-utensils` (its `.popis` holds "V kombinaci s hotelem …" instead), so every Thai stay comes back unknown. Verified mismatch: adapter emits board=unknown for `https://www.esotravel.cz/pobytove/thajsko/hotel/sea-breeze-resort-pattaya/?ha=1077,24&zj=26`, but that page's term table reads "15 dní / 12 nocí | 1 | snídaně | 36 990,-" and "Cena zahrnuje: … 12x ubytování se snídaní" → real board is BB.

### 7.

FIELD, stars can exceed 5 and half-stars are silently dropped. `stars = card.find('h2 i.fa-star').length` (src/sources/esotravel.ts:210) returned 6 for Constance Halaveli because ESO renders six `fa-star` glyphs for that resort (verified in the h1 of `https://www.esotravel.cz/pobytove/maledivy/hotel/constance-halaveli-maledivy-severni-ari-atol/?ha=1787&zj=470`) — an out-of-range value for a 1-5 star field. Conversely `fa-star-half` is a different class token and is not counted, so `Hotel Villas Mon Plaisir` (2 x fa-star + fa-star-half) and the many 3.5-star Thai hotels round DOWN silently.

### 8.

FIELD, transport unknown 100% and departureAirport NULL 100% — site-imposed under the current 1-GET design, but every offer is in fact a flight package from Prague. `.tour-type` never contains "letec": its icon is `fab fa-fort-awesome` for both stays and tours (30-60 per listing page), and `fas fa-plane` appears only on the single "Exclusive zájezd" private-jet card, so the guarded regex at src/sources/esotravel.ts:216 can never fire. The information lives on the offer page — "Cena zahrnuje: leteckou přepravu Praha - Bangkok - Praha" (sea-breeze URL above), "leteckou přepravu Praha - Malé - Praha s přestupem" (halaveli URL above) → transport=flight, departureAirport=PRG for essentially the whole source.

### 9.

COSMETIC — the docstring's sitemap claim is stale. src/sources/esotravel.ts:20-22 says country listings are `/dovolena/{slug}/zajezdy/zajezdy/` (DOUBLE zajezdy) "actually" per the sitemap; the live `sitemap.xml` today contains 148 single-`zajezdy` URLs (`https://www.esotravel.cz/dovolena/mauritius/zajezdy/`) and zero double ones. Functionally harmless — I fetched both shapes for mauritius and got byte-comparable pages (17 cards each) — but the comment now points a future maintainer at the wrong evidence.

## Adversariální ověření

- **POTVRZENO** (high): CONFIRMED. I tried to refute this on all four false-positive grounds and every one failed.

WHAT I CHECKED INDEPENDENTLY (own curl, generic Chrome UA, >=4s gaps; own tsx harness against the real parseEsoListing export):

1. Mechanism — fetched https://www.esotravel.cz/js/redesign/pageloader.js myself (HTTP 200, 3145 B). It is verbatim as reported: `this.preload()` does `$.ajax({ url: this.path + constructGetUrl({ strana: this.page }) })`, slices `new XRegExp('<!-- startresults ' + self.feed.attr('id') + ' -->.+?<!-- endresults -->')` out of the response, and stops when `XRegExp('<!-- feed_end -->')` matches. `.load-more` calls `display()` which appends that content and calls `preload()` again. On both listings the `.feed` element has NO `data-feed-path`, so `this.path = ''` and the AJAX URL resolves relative to the current listing — exactly the `?strana=N` URLs used below. So "Načíst další" DOES issue an extra request. src/sources/esotravel.ts:9-10 ("only reveals already-embedded cards (no extra request), so a single GET per listing sees everything") and the repeat of that claim in the parseEsoListing docstring at src/sources/esotravel.ts:258-259 ("the ones behind 'Načíst další' are present in the HTML") are both factually wrong.

2.
