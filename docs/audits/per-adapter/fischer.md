# Audit: fischer (high)

## Shrnutí

The adapter (/Users/daniel/Library/CloudStorage/OneDrive-Osobní/Effistream/dovolenky/src/sources/fischer.ts) does one GET of https://www.fischer.cz/last-minute, parses the server-rendered hydration blob in div[data-component-name="appTourList"] > script[type="application/json"], sorts the tours by departureDate ascending, takes the 10 earliest (MAX_TOURS=10) and for each POSTs /api/TourList/getTourHotelList with hotelsCountToGet=5 (HOTELS_PER_TOUR=5). Field mapping is genuinely correct — I verified every field of two separate tours against the site's own rendered result cards and found zero mismatches: pricePerPerson really is per-adult, all-in with flight, for the stated number of nights; departureDate, nights, board, stars, country and locality all match. The problem is volume, not accuracy. The hydration blob itself reports tourListResult.totalCount = 200 tours while embedding only the first 20, so the adapter surfaces 10 of 200 tours (5%), and within each tour 5 of up to 19 hotels — roughly 1–3% of Fischer's own last-minute offer space, 40 offers in total. Because it takes the *earliest-departing* 10, the output collapses to a 4-day departure window (2026-07-31 … 2026-08-03) and 4 countries, while the full last-minute list spans 2026-07-29 … 2026-08-14, 6 countries and a 9 590 – 132 240 CZK tour price range. The paginated endpoint that would fix this already works and needs the documentGuid the adapter already parses and then throws away. Separately, /last-minute is a beach-charter-only section, so Fischer's entire exotic/long-haul catalogue (Maledivy, Mauricius, Seychely, Dominikána, Mexiko, SAE, Jamajka, Keňa, Omán …), first-minute and poznávací zájezdy never reach the board via this source at all. Bottom line: Fischer is present and the numbers it reports are trustworthy, but it is contributing a token sample rather than its inventory.

## Nálezy k opravě

### 1.

COVERAGE, primary: the adapter reads only the 20 tours embedded in the /last-minute hydration and then keeps just 10 of them (MAX_TOURS=10), while the same blob states tourListResult.totalCount = 200. The remaining 180 tours are reachable through POST https://www.fischer.cz/api/TourList/getTourList with body {documentGuid, searchSettings:{searchFromIndex, toursCountToGet:20, sortOrder:'asc', sortBy:''}} — I verified it live at searchFromIndex 20/60/100/140/180, all HTTP 200. The irony is that parseFischerHydration() already extracts documentGuid (fischer.ts:111, asserted in tests/fischer.test.ts:18) and fetchOffers never reads it; the guid is even stable (today's live value 04af28e1-df72-4dc0-894e-6548566d67dc is identical to the one in the committed fixture). Net effect: 5% of the last-minute tour list is scanned.

### 2.

COVERAGE: because the 10 tours are picked earliest-departing-first, the live output covers departure dates 2026-07-31 (20 offers), 08-01 (5), 08-02 (5), 08-03 (10) — a 4-day window — and only 4 countries (Španělsko 15, Řecko 15, Tunisko 5, Bulharsko 5). Sampling 120 of the 200 last-minute tours via getTourList shows the real section spans 2026-07-29 → 2026-08-14 and 6 countries: Řecko 38, Španělsko 25, Tunisko 21, Bulharsko 17, Egypt 12, Kapverdské ostrovy 7. Egypt and Kapverdy never appear in the adapter's output at all, despite Egypt being 10% of the last-minute list.

### 3.

COVERAGE: the tour-level price band is truncated. Across the 120 sampled tours adultPriceFrom runs 9 590 – 132 240 CZK (top entries: Řecko/Paxos 132 240, Španělsko/Menorca 14 nights 79 860, Řecko/Soluň 74 540 / 74 230, Menorca 14 nights 51 990, Kos 14 nights 43 850 z Pardubic). Not one of those tours is inside the adapter's 4-day window — its own tour ceiling is 33 990 (its 87 290 max offer comes from an expensive hotel inside a cheap Costa Brava/Kos tour). The whole 34k–132k tour band is structurally invisible.

### 4.

COVERAGE: HOTELS_PER_TOUR=5 is a second, multiplicative cut. Re-issuing getTourHotelList for tour 869681786 (Řecko/Kos, 2026-07-31, 4 nights) with hotelsCountToGet=50 returned hotelsTotalCount=19 and all 19 hotels — the adapter takes the first 5 and drops 14, including Grecotel Casa Paradiso 34 360, MITSIS SELECTION BLUE DOMES 40 560, Aqua Blu Boutique 50 440 and Grecotel Luxme Kos 47 230. Combined with the tour cut this is ~10/200 × ~5/19 ≈ 1.3% of the section's offer space.

### 5.

COVERAGE, scope: /last-minute is a summer beach-charter section only. Fischer's own navigation on that same page links dedicated catalogue pages for /maledivy, /mauricius, /seychely, /dominikanska-republika, /mexiko, /jamajka, /panama, /kena, /kambodza, /oman, /katar, /bahrajn, /saudska-arabie, /spojene-arabske-emiraty, plus the sections /exoticka-dovolena, /exotika-primym-letem, /first-minute, /akcni-nabidky, /poznavaci-zajezdy and /eurovikendy. None of those countries occur in any of the 120 last-minute tours I sampled, so Fischer's entire exotic/long-haul inventory — the 40–150k CZK band the brand is best known for — is absent from the board through this source. Those pages use a different component (appHotelTourCards, empty on first paint) or the /searchresult/getsearch endpoint rather than appTourList, so they would need separate wiring; a plain getsearch call with tt=1&nn=7|8|…|14 returned prices up to 84 990 CZK.

### 6.

FIELD: departureAirport is hardcoded to null (fischer.ts:164) even though every tour object carries departureLocation with the real Czech departure city. Live values across the sampled list: Praha, Brno, Ostrava, Pardubice, České Budějovice. toTourMeta() (fischer.ts:179-189) simply never reads the property. Free data being discarded.

### 7.

FIELD/dedup: sourceOfferKey = offerKeyHash([hotelId, departureDate, nights, board]) (fischer.ts:150) omits the departure city, so two genuinely different offers collide and mapFischerHotels/fetchOffers silently keep only the first. This is not hypothetical — the live list contains both 'Řecko | Kos | 2026-07-31 | 11 nights | Ostrava' (tour -1515308234) and 'Řecko | Kos | 2026-07-31 | 11 nights | Praha', each adultPriceFrom 33 990. Today the bug is masked only because the 10-tour cap rarely admits both; it becomes real as soon as pagination is added. Fix the key together with the departureAirport mapping.

### 8.

FIELD, low: claimedOriginalPrice / claimedDiscountPct are always null although Fischer publishes both explicitly on the result card. For https://www.fischer.cz/spanelsko/costa-brava/malgrat-de-mar/sorra-daurada-splash (31.7.–11.8.2026, 11 nights, 2 adults) the site prints 'Původní cena 86 620 Kč, Sleva 26 640 Kč' against 'Celkem 59 980 Kč' — i.e. 43 310 CZK/person original, ≈30.7% off. priceTotal is likewise null while the site states 'Celkem 59 980 Kč'. The file header documents this as a deliberate v1 deferral, so it is a design decision rather than a defect, but the data is one request away.

### 9.

DOC, cosmetic: the header comment (fischer.ts:26-27) states nightsCount is always {from:7,to:7} 'since /last-minute is fixed at a 7-night stay'. Live data contradicts it — 4-4, 7-7, 11-11 and 14-14 all occur. No functional impact (from === to in every observed tour, so the `nightsCount.from` collapse at fischer.ts:185 is still correct), but the stated invariant is stale.
