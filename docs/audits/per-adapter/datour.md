# Audit: datour (high)

## Shrnutí

src/sources/datour.ts queries GET https://search.anchoice.cz/web-search?page=1&location=<id>&package=0 (Referer: https://datour.cz/) once per country for 12 hardcoded exotic location ids, parses the JSON `packages[]` directly, keeps the cheapest room variant per (tour_id, start, nights, board_id) and dedupes globally. It ran clean live: 210 offers in 49.5s across 12/12 successful queries, prices 3 170–77 990 CZK/person (median 27 305), 0 nulls on title/country/departureDate/nights/price/tourOperator. Pricing and the core descriptive fields are CORRECT — I verified unit_price is genuinely per-person against the site's own badge ("Cena za osobu od 22 529 Kč" = adapter 22529), plus stars/board/transport/country/locality on the same offer. Two real problems. (1) The offer URL is wrong for 100% of offers: the adapter emits https://datour.cz/{detail}, which is a hard Next.js 404 ("Tady žádné zájezdy ani informace nejsou…"); the working route is https://datour.cz/zajezd/{detail}. Every board row and Telegram link for this source is dead. (2) Coverage is ~10%: the adapter fetches only page 1 (18 rows) per country, so it sees 210 of the 1 845 offers its own 12 ids match, and it omits 12 more countries that the `exotika` watch profile explicitly asks for — including Keňa and Filipíny, whose ids the spec had already verified. Net, the adapter surfaces ~9.7% of the datour inventory that the user's profile wants.

## Nálezy k opravě

### 1.

BROKEN OFFER URL — 100% of offers link to a 404. mapPackage builds `https://datour.cz/${detail}` (datour.ts:204, DETAIL_URL_BASE line 87). datour.cz is a Next.js catch-all that returns HTTP 200 for any path, so a status check passes, but __NEXT_DATA__ reports `props.pageProps.notFound: true` and the rendered page is the site's 404 ("Tady žádné zájezdy ani informace nejsou..."). Verified on 3 offers: https://datour.cz/maledivy/ari-atoll/-ari-atol-jih-/liberty-guesthouse-maldives, https://datour.cz/kuba/to-nejlepsi-z-kuby, https://datour.cz/dominikanska-republika/dominikanska-republika-barbados-z-la-romany-na-lodi-msc-opera — all notFound:true. The working route is `https://datour.cz/zajezd/{detail}` (also `/hotel/{detail}`), which resolves to the Next.js page `/detail-hotel` with real pageProps (data, hotel_url, termQuery) and renders the offer. The module header's justification is wrong: it argues the router echoing `subpage: [maledivy, ari-atoll, …]` proves canonicity, but that echo is exactly what the catch-all 404 route does with any path. 0/210 live rows lack a `detail` slug, so the `vyhledavani?location=` fallback never fires and never masks this.

### 2.

COVERAGE — only page 1 is fetched, so ~89% of the matched inventory is invisible. buildQueries() hardcodes `page=1` (datour.ts:160). Live `total` vs rows returned: Thajsko 508→18, SAE 265→18, Dominikánská 198→18, Maledivy 187→18, Mexiko 139→18, Srí Lanka 117→18, Indonésie 106→18, Mauricius 103→18, Zanzibar 84→17, Vietnam 71→18, Seychely 53→17, Kuba 14→14. That is 210 of 1 845 = 11.4%. The endpoint sorts price-ascending (verified: Maledivy page 1 = 22529…31889, page 2 of Thajsko continues 19759…20935 straight after page 1's 15839…19690), so the adapter does get the cheapest slice — but each country is silently truncated at an arbitrary price ceiling well under the profile's 60 000 CZK cap: Thajsko is cut at 19 690, SAE at 13 635, Srí Lanka at 25 695, Dominikánská at 14 740. Every Thai package between 19 690 and 60 000 CZK is unreachable. page=2 works with the same params, so this is a cheap fix.

### 3.

COVERAGE — 12 of the 24 countries in the `exotika` watch profile have ZERO coverage even though live location ids exist. LOCATION_IDS (datour.ts:93-106) has 12 entries; the site's own https://search.anchoice.cz/filter-location returns 172 top-level countries with ids. Missing, with live `total`: Japonsko 112 (id 29513), Keňa 40 (27990), Kapverdské ostrovy 37 (452557), Jihoafrická republika 32 (28422), Filipíny 27 (29724), Réunion 24 (567314), Kambodža 23 (599485), Nepál 7 (30211), Peru 5 (29246), Namibie 3 (28408), Tanzanie 1 (452666), Madagaskar 1 (27998) — 312 offers, none seen. Keňa 27990 and Filipíny 29724 are the worst case: spec §16.1 row 16 lists both as live-verified ids, but the adapter shipped with only 12 of the 14 the spec had confirmed. Counting these, the adapter returns 210 of 2 157 profile-relevant offers = 9.7%.

### 4.

FIELD — departureAirport is hardcoded null (datour.ts:229) although `departure_location_name` is populated on 89% of live rows, and 24% of offers do NOT depart from Czechia: across the 210 live rows the split is Praha 138, Vídeň 43, Bratislava 7, Mnichov 1, null 21. The verified Maledivy offer (https://datour.cz/zajezd/maledivy/ari-atoll/-ari-atol-jih-/liberty-guesthouse-maldives) is a Vídeň departure at 22 529 CZK; the site itself renders the airport chips "Letecky Praha Vídeň". Dropping it means 51 offers look CZ-departure-equivalent on the board when they need a drive to Vienna/Bratislava/Munich — and it makes datour prices not like-for-like against sources that do carry the airport.

### 5.

FIELD — half-star categories are rounded UP, inflating stars on 7.6% of rows. `round(parseFloat(accommodation_category))` (datour.ts:192-194) turns '3.5' into 4. Live distribution: 4.0×89, 3.0×66, 5.0×33, 3.5×15, 2.0×4, 4.5×1, null×2 — 16/210 rows carry a .5 value. Concrete mismatch: https://datour.cz/zajezd/dominikanska-republika/dominikanska-republika-barbados-z-la-romany-na-lodi-msc-opera has `accommodation_category: '3.5'` in web-search → adapter emits stars=4, but the offer's own detail page reports `hotel_category: 3`. The platform truncates, the adapter rounds up.

### 6.

DATA SHAPE (not an adapter bug, but it distorts the board) — the two cheapest exotic offers the adapter returns are cruise legs without airfare. `Dominikánská republika, Barbados z La Romany na lodi MSC Opera`, 2026-11-16, 3 nights, 3 170 CZK/person, transport_name 'Vlastní' → transport 'own', provider PT Tours. 20 of 210 rows are 'Vlastní'. The mapping is faithful, but the `exotika` profile deliberately omits the transport filter (config/watch.yaml comment: exotic countries are inherently flights), so a 3 170 CZK Caribbean cruise segment that does not include getting to La Romana will rank as datour's headline exotic bargain.

### 7.

INFO — claimedOriginalPrice, claimedDiscountPct and priceTotal are null on 210/210 live offers. Confirmed at the source: `original_price: 0.0`, `package_discount: 0.0`, `package_price: 0.0`, `package_prices: []` on every row I inspected. The adapter's guards are correct and this matches its documented expectation, but it means datour can never contribute a claimed discount — every datour alert must come from our own 30-day price history, and the discount-treated branch of mapPackage is entirely unexercised in production.
