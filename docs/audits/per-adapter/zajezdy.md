# Audit: zajezdy (high)

## Shrnutí

The adapter GETs 12 hardcoded SSR listing pages on https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/<slug>/ (recko, turecko, egypt, chorvatsko, bulharsko, all-inclusive, letecky-praha + 5 exotic: thajsko, maledivy, mauricius, spojene-arabske-emiraty, dominikanska-republika), extracts the embedded `window.searchData` JSON with a balanced-brace scan, and emits one offer per tourResults[].departures[] entry. Live run: 294 offers / 100 unique tours in 68 s, prices 7 633–173 070 CZK, 10 countries, all 294 with non-null date/nights/board/country, 0 stale dates. Price mapping is right — `totalAdultPrice.amount` really is per-person all-in (verified against three detail pages showing "Kč/os" for "2 dospělí"), and the discount derivation is exact (poSleve 15 % → 8 980 vs the page's own `cataloguePrice: 8980.00`). Bottom line: not broken, but two real defects. (1) `nights` is a raw date difference, which is correct for short-haul charter/own-car but over-counts by 1–2 on every long-haul and every bus departure — ~155/294 offers (53 %), and `nights` feeds the per-night discount engine, the market nights-band bucketing and the cross-source dedupe key. (2) Coverage: 7 country slugs that exist on the very same host and sit in the site's own top nav are absent entirely, including Itálie — the site's single largest last-minute market (6 393 tours) — and Španělsko (2 527); depth is 100 of 23 046 last-minute tours and 3 of up to 674 departures per tour, sampled in the site's "Doporučení" order rather than by price.

## Nálezy k opravě

### 1.

nights is over-counted by 1-2 on every long-haul and every bus departure (~155/294 offers, 53%): the adapter takes the plain date difference from `odjezdPrijezd`, but the site's own detail page states the real hotel-night count. Verified 5/5 mismatches: Heritance Aarah/Maledivy adapter nights=14 vs page "15 dní, 12 nocí" (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-heritance-aarah-raa-atol-maledivy-z2673258/2883044601/); Hyatt Zilara Cap Cana/Dominikánská adapter 8 vs "9 dní, 7 nocí" (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-hyatt-zilara-cap-cana-punta-cana-dominikanska-republika-z2818636/2887152581/); Burasari Phuket/Thajsko adapter 13 vs "14 dní, 12 nocí" (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-burasari-phuket-andamanske-more-thajsko-z3059421/2836136161/); The Retreat Palm Dubai/SAE adapter 8 vs "9 dní, 7 nocí" (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-the-retreat-palm-dubai-dubaj-spojene-arabske-emiraty-z3163622/2861294995/); Pokoje LJUBA/Chorvatsko autobusem adapter 9 vs "10 dní, 7 nocí" (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-pokoje-s-polopenzi-ljuba-makarska-riviera-chorvatsko-z3217566/2831967506/). Short-haul is fine (3/3 correct). Impact is not cosmetic: src/core/discount.ts computes `input.current / nights`, so a 9-instead-of-7 offer looks 22 % cheaper per night than it is and can fire a false discount; src/core/market.ts nightsBand() drops it from the 6-8 band into 9-12 so it is compared against the wrong market bucket; computeDedupeKey/computeMatchKey in src/core/normalize.ts include nights, so the same trip from another source will not dedupe; format.ts prints the wrong "(N nocí)" to Telegram.

### 2.

Seven country slugs that exist on the same host, return HTTP 200 with a full 10-tour `window.searchData`, and are linked from the site's own top nav are missing from SLUGS entirely — so those countries produce zero offers on the board. Live-probed tourCount / price span of the 10 returned tours: italie 6 393 tours (11 985-34 180 CZK), spanelsko 2 527 (17 790-34 516), tunisko 526 (15 341-27 249), kypr 508 (20 393-52 334), portugalsko 406 (23 139-81 810), cerna-hora 165 (15 990-39 990), albanie 87 (16 590-29 690). Itálie alone has more last-minute tours than Řecko (4 046), which IS covered. The adapter's live output contains only 10 countries: Egypt 31, Turecko 30, Chorvatsko 30, Maledivy 30, Mauricius 30, SAE 30, Řecko 29, Bulharsko 29, Dominikánská 28, Thajsko 27. Also unused: /letecky-brno/ and /letecky-ostrava/ (only /letecky-praha/ is in SLUGS).

### 3.

Depth is ~0.4 % of the agency's last-minute inventory and the sample is promoted-order, not price-order. https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-u-more/ reports tourCount=23 046; the adapter returns 100 unique tours / 294 departures. Per tour it takes the 3 departures the SSR page ships while the JSON's own `departuresCount` goes up to 674 (Eri Beach Resort, Řecko) with `hasMore:true` on all 10 tours. `?page=` really is Disallowed in robots (confirmed under `User-agent: *`), so pagination is legitimately closed — but the same JSON exposes `locationFacets`, 42 clean robots-allowed locality slugs for Řecko alone (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/kreta/, /rhodos/, /korfu/, /zakynthos/ …), which is an unused breadth lever. On top of that the listing renders "Řadit podle: Doporučení" — the 10 tours are the site's recommended order, so for a discount board the cheap / high-discount tail is systematically skipped (`?sort=` is not in the Disallow list).

### 4.

transport is 'unknown' for 30/294 offers (all Croatian) even though the source JSON labels them explicitly. buildOffer derives transport solely from `departure.letiste`, but each departure also carries `doprava` (5=autem, 1=autobusem, 2=letecky) and `transportLabel` ("autem" / "autobusem" / "letecky – Brno"). In the live /chorvatsko/ payload that is 20 × doprava=5 and 10 × doprava=1. The detail pages confirm the labels: Penzion BARTULOVIĆ shows "Doprava: Autem" (https://last-minute.zajezdy.cz/dovolena-penzion-bartulovic-makarska-riviera-chorvatsko-z2610188/2632055594/) and Pokoje LJUBA shows "Doprava: Autobusem". These should map to 'own' and 'bus'. Note normalizeTransport() in src/core/normalize.ts would still return 'unknown' for the literal string "autem" (it only matches vlastni/own and autobus/bus), so mapping the numeric `doprava` code is the safer fix.

## Adversariální ověření

- **VYVRÁCENO — neopravovat** (low): I re-probed the site and the live board myself, and the finding's facts hold but its framing as a defect does not.

WHAT I CONFIRMED LIVE (Chrome 126 UA, >=6.5 s apart, 3 requests total to last-minute.zajezdy.cz):
- /italie/ → HTTP 200, tourCount 6394, tourResults=10, 30 departures, countryName "Itálie", 11 985–34 180 CZK (report said 6393 / same span — pure inventory drift).
- /kypr/ → HTTP 200, tourCount 508, 10 tours, 20 393–52 334 CZK — exact match.
- /letecky-brno/ → HTTP 200, 10 tours, but all ten are Řecko (already covered by /recko/), 26 departures.
- Every one of the seven slugs plus both airport slugs is href-linked on each page (nav check hit /italie/, /spanelsko/, /kypr/, /tunisko/, /albanie/, /letecky-brno/, /letecky-ostrava/).
- Řecko tourCount 4043 vs Itálie 6394 — the "Italy has more inventory than a covered country" claim is true.
- SLUGS in src/sources/zajezdy.ts (lines 20-34) contains none of them. True.

WHY IT IS NOT A DEFECT:

1) The impact claim is empirically false for 6 of the 7 countries. I pulled the live board (GET https://dovolenky-rho.vercel.app/api/offers, 1513 active offers) and counted by country: Itálie 10 (cedok 5, alexandria 2, invia 1, bluestyle 1, dovolenkovani 1), Španělsko 18 (fischer 15, bluestyle 2, alexandria 1), Tunisko 7 (fischer 5, bluestyle 2), Albánie 2, Černá Hora 1, Portugalsko 1. Only Kypr is genuinely 0 board-wide. "Those countries produce zero offers on the board" is wrong for six of seven. (Bulharsko and Egypt are 0 on the board for an unrelated reason: GET /api/exclusions returns ["Bulharsko","Egypt"] — the user's own negative filter — so two of the twelve slugs currently feed countries the user has globally hidden.)

2) The omission is documented intentional design — false-positive pattern (c). The file header states SLUGS is a fixed recon-confirmed set and that even live-verified slugs were "OMITTED to keep SLUGS ≤ 12 (5s gap → ~60 s/scan politeness ceiling, spec §16.2)". docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-04-dovolenky-design.md §16.2 says "strop celkem ~12 slugů (5s gap → ~60 s/scan)", §3 row 6 defines the strategy as breadth over a slug set, and the run-budget line pins "zajezdy 12×5s". SLUGS is exactly 12 — sitting on the documented ceiling, not accidentally short of it.

3) The literal remedy would break the source. src/core/run.ts has a hard ADAPTER_FETCH_TIMEOUT_MS = 120000, on timeout the whole adapter is recorded 'failed' and contributes nothing. From the auditor's own measurement (12 slugs, 67.7 s, 5 s host gap) per-request latency is ~1.06 s. Adding the 7 country slugs → 19 slugs ≈ 110 s (one retry or one slow 500 KB page from the ceiling); adding the two airport slugs as well → 21 slugs ≈ 122 s, i.e. over the timeout, converting today's 294 offers into 0.

4) The airport half of the finding is weak on its own evidence: my /letecky-brno/ probe returned ten Řecko tours, so it buys departure-airport variety on already-covered countries, not country breadth — the exact overlap the ≤12 cap exists to arbitrate.

WHAT SURVIVES (why severity low rather than none): Kypr really is zero across all 15 sources while config/watch.yaml's leto-more profile explicitly watches Řecko, Turecko, Egypt, Španělsko, Kypr, Bulharsko, Chorvatsko, Itálie — the adapter covers 5 of those 8, and two of its twelve slots (egypt, bulharsko) currently harvest countries the user excludes at display time. So a budget-neutral SWAP (e.g. egypt/bulharsko → kypr/italie) is a defensible enhancement. But that is a product/allocation suggestion, not the reported bug: nothing here misparses, mis-prices, or silently drops data, the behavior matches both the module header and the spec, and the finding's stated consequence does not reproduce.

- **POTVRZENO** (medium): CONFIRMED — the core claim survives independent verification, though the auditor's scope framing ("every long-haul") is wrong.

WHAT THE CODE DOES: parseOdjezdPrijezd() (src/sources/zajezdy.ts:142-169) parses the two dates in `odjezdPrijezd` and sets nights = Math.round((endMs - startMs)/86400000) — the plain calendar span of the trip, i.e. the departure-to-return-day difference. buildOffer() (line 204) puts that straight into NormalizedOffer.nights. The RawDeparture interface declares `delka` (line 65) but never reads it; I checked and it would not help anyway: `delka` is "(N dní)" and across both repo fixtures plus both live listing pages I fetched, delka days == diff + 1 in every single row, so it carries zero extra information.

LIVE EVIDENCE (my own URLs, not the auditor's; generic Chrome 126 UA, >=6 s apart, 6 requests total, inside the 08-24 Prague window):
- Heritance Aarah / Maledivy, listing "Pá 7. 8. – Pá 21. 8." delka (15 dní), 173 070 Kč → adapter nights 14. Detail page for that exact departure id (same dates, same 173 070 Kč, same AI board, same Praha): "Termín 7. 8. – 21. 8. 2026 15 dní, 12 nocí". Off by 2.
- Sandies Bathala / Maledivy, "So 22. 8. – Po 31. 8." (10 dní), 88 310 Kč → adapter 9. Detail: "10 dní, 7 nocí". Off by 2.
- Vila Juroš / Chorvatsko autobusem, "Pá 7. 8. – Ne 16. 8." (10 dní), 11 850 Kč → adapter 9. Detail: "10 dní, 7 nocí", "Doprava Autobusem". Off by 2. On the /chorvatsko/ listing ALL 9 bus departures are (10 dní)/diff 9, so bus is systematically +2.
- Counter-case: KANDIMA MALDIVES "St 12. 8. – St 19. 8." (8 dní) → adapter 7; detail says "8 dní, 7 nocí" ✓. So it is NOT "every long-haul" — the discriminator is whether the term carries overnight/travel-day overhead (bus, and long-haul terms whose delka != nights+1), not haul length. The auditor's 155/294 (53%) is therefore unverified as stated, but plausible in magnitude: on /maledivy/ 24 of 30 terms have delka >= 9 dní, on /chorvatsko/ 9 of 30 are bus.

SEMANTICS ARE NOT AMBIGUOUS: every other adapter emits hotel nights — bluestyle `term.nightCount`, cesys `duration_night`, alexandria/datour `nights`, der `nightsCount`, adventura/cedok explicitly `days - 1`. cesys.ts:60-70 even documents the project's awareness that the API's calendar-days field and duration_night "aren't a fixed offset apart (diff observed as 1 *or* 2 nights depending on flight times)". So zajezdy is the odd one out, and format.ts:122 prints this number to Telegram as "(N nocí)".

FALSE-POSITIVE CHECKS, all ruled out: (a) not a "field the source doesn't publish" in the exculpating sense — I dumped every key of window.searchData (top level, tourResult, tour, departure, options) and there is no nights field anywhere, but the adapter does not emit null, it emits a confidently wrong number that downstream treats as hotel nights; (b) not a teaser/term mismatch — each detail page matched the listing on date range, price, board and departure airport, and the URL is the departure-specific one; (c) no intentional-design note — the parseOdjezdPrijezd docstring calls the value "nights" with no caveat, and tests/zajezdy.test.ts:75 only asserts 7/11 from an all-short-haul fixture, so the bug is untested rather than sanctioned; (d) not a dedupe artifact.

DOWNSTREAM IMPACT VERIFIED IN CODE: discount.ts:108 currentPN = round(current/nights) — an inflated 9-instead-of-7 understates per-night price by 22 %; market.ts:13-18 nightsBand moves 7→"6-8" into 9→"9-12" so the market pool is the wrong bucket; hotelTermPricesPN (market.ts:139-175) matches by hotel_key across ALL sources with |nights - offer.nights| <= 2, so a zajezdy 9 still pools with another source's true 7 and then loses 22 % on the per-night comparison — a manufactured "real discount"; normalize.ts:158 computeMatchKey includes nights so cross-source twins won't collapse; digest.ts ranks the Telegram top-10 by realPct, so the inflated offers get promoted.

Severity medium rather than high: it is one source of 16, the bias is uniform within zajezdy so same-bucket zajezdy-vs-zajezdy comparisons largely cancel, and the false-discount path needs cross-source overlap on the same hotel/locality. It is not cosmetic though — it silently biases the product's flagship metric upward and prints a wrong night count to users.
